About NIST University

Established in 1994 in Berhampur, Odisha

College Overview

National Institute of Science & Technology (NIST) University is an engineering institution located in Berhampur, Odisha. Established in 1994, the university provides undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degrees in various engineering and technology branches.

Mission

To provide strong academic knowledge and practical skills to help students excel in technological innovation and professional fields.

Vision

To be recognized as a leading technical university providing high-quality education and research for societal development.

Courses Offered

Undergraduate and Postgraduate Academic Programs

B.Tech CSE

Computer Science & Engineering focuses on programming languages, software development, data structures, and computer networking.

  • Duration: 4 Years
  • Eligibility: 10+2 with Science (PCM)
  • Seats: 180

B.Tech ECE

Electronics & Communication Engineering covers electronic circuits, signal processing, wireless communication, and embedded systems.

  • Duration: 4 Years
  • Eligibility: 10+2 with Science (PCM)
  • Seats: 120

B.Tech EEE

Electrical & Electronics Engineering deals with electrical power generation, electrical machinery, circuit design, and control systems.

  • Duration: 4 Years
  • Eligibility: 10+2 with Science (PCM)
  • Seats: 60

MCA

Master of Computer Applications is a postgraduate degree in advanced computer application development, database management, and web applications.

  • Duration: 2 Years
  • Eligibility: Graduation with Mathematics
  • Seats: 60
